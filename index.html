Observed Patterns

Three structures
in every campus.

Pattern 01

Social Clusters

Students don't interact uniformly. They self-organize into tight groups — friend clusters, study groups, social circles — that persist across weeks and locations.

Pattern 02

Movement Corridors

Campus movement isn't random. Students trace predictable arcs between fixed locations, creating high-traffic corridors that define the social geography of the campus.

Pattern 03

Interaction Density

Some locations act as social magnets. Certain spaces on campus accumulate disproportionate numbers of interactions — invisible hotspots of connection and community.
